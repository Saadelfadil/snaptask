Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

SnapTask does one thing exceptionally well — it turns the text in your screenshots into the actions you actually want to take.

On-Device OCR

Powered by Google's bundled ML Kit text recognition model. All reading happens locally — your screenshots never leave your phone.

Smart Entity Detection

Automatically identifies dates, times, phone numbers, emails, URLs, addresses, and prices — from any screenshot content.

Fully Offline

SnapTask requires no internet connection. The app declares no INTERNET permission — it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

Smart Reminders

Spotted a date in a screenshot? SnapTask creates a reminder with the right time, title, and context — with one tap.

Privacy-First

No account. No tracking. No analytics. No ads. Your screenshots stay on your device — period.

No Account Required

Open the app and start using it immediately. No sign-up, no login, no email. Just productivity.

How It Works

From screenshot to action
in seconds.

No typing. No copying. No switching apps. Just share and go.

  1. 01

    Share a screenshot

    Take a screenshot of anything — a conversation, a document, a booking confirmation, a recipe, anything. Then tap Share → SnapTask.

  2. 02

    Text is extracted instantly

    The on-device ML Kit model reads every word in your screenshot in under a second — no internet, no waiting.

  3. 03

    Key information is detected

    SnapTask identifies dates, phone numbers, emails, links, addresses, and prices — and highlights them with confidence scoring.

  4. 04

    Take action with one tap

    SnapTask ranks and presents the right action for each detected item. Set a reminder, open a link, call a number, or send an email — instantly.
